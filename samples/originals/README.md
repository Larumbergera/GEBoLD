# Original sample frames

A few unmodified frames from [GI4E](https://www.unavarra.es/gi4e/databases/gi4e?languageId=1) and [I2Head](https://www.unavarra.es/gi4e/databases/i2head?languageId=1), with their six-landmark annotations (`.pts`, 1-based coordinates). They are the original images of some of the predictions shown in the sibling folders, and the tests use them to check the full pipeline.

They belong to the [GI4E group databases](https://www.unavarra.es/gi4e/databases?languageId=1) of the Public University of Navarre, which distributes the complete databases on request; they are included here only as a small sample.
